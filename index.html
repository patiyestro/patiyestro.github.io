About the Artist

Pati Yestro

Pati Yestro is a contemporary Rwandan artist based in Kigali, working primarily with oil and mixed media.

His practice explores memory, identity, resilience, transformation, and the emotional stories carried by the human body.

Women and children frequently occupy the center of his work, reflecting themes of strength, vulnerability, beauty, memory, and the experiences that shape us.

Through painting, Pati seeks to communicate what words sometimes cannot.

Artist Statement

“I want to change the world without talking, only using my hands.”
