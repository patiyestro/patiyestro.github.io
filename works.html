The Price of Becoming Human

Series:
What Survived Me
Title:
The Price of Becoming Human
Size:
180 × 110 cm
Medium:
Oil on canvas
Year:
2024
Description

This is the story of life's endless chain
Of love, of loss, of joy and pain
We fall and rise again, reborn
Life's never ending reincarnation

We come into this world as nothing
And slowly learn to know everything
The love we carry, the losses we bear
The dreams we chase and pay for with our tears

We fall and fracture, change and grow
And leave behind us fragments of long ago
But always something remains
A memory, a scar, a flower
A trace of some long gone life
That lives within our next birth.

Around us we see life's cycle
Of lives that turn into memories
That turn into stories and then
Become part of somebody's next life.

Maybe becoming human isn't
About learning to escape all grief
And loss and pay and tears and broken leaves
But learning to carry all these things
And still become. This seems to be

The price of being human.
